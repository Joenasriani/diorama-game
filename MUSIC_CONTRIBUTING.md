# Contributing music to DIORAMA

DIORAMA welcomes **proposals** from musicians and sound designers who want to contribute original music to its miniature mechanical-puzzle world.

**Creative approval is mandatory.** A submission does not grant permission to add a track to the game. The DIORAMA maintainer (Joe Nasr) listens to and approves each track before it can be considered for inclusion. Maintaining the game's existing playful, gentle, cohesive mood takes priority over increasing track count.

## How to submit

1. Open a [Music submission issue](https://github.com/Joenasriani/diorama-game/issues/new/choose).
2. Provide a streaming/listening-preview link (not an unreviewed audio file committed to the repository), track title, artist/stage name for credits, length, loop behavior, and mood/creative notes.
3. Declare whether you created the entire recording/composition and identify samples, collaborators, third-party material, and any AI-generated elements whose licensing or provenance needs review.
4. Specify the license/permissions you can offer for inclusion in the browser game, hosting and redistribution of an approved audio file, playback in trailers, and attribution. A streaming link alone is **not** permission to redistribute audio.
5. Await rights/technical screening, then Joe Nasr's explicit creative approval. If requested, provide a private review file by an agreed channel; don't publish proprietary audio to issues without permission.

## Musical direction

The current HitsLab track remains the reference and default. Aim for playful, thoughtful, light and unobtrusive music that supports puzzle concentration and repeats comfortably. Avoid abrupt changes in loudness, aggressive drops, distracting vocals, jarring transitions or a style inconsistent with the game. These are selection preferences, not an automatic rejection algorithm.

## Review criteria

- **Rights:** identifiable copyright holders, lawful sample usage, permission to distribute within the public browser game and source package, and suitable license terms documented before acceptance.
- **Creative:** atmosphere, cohesion with existing music, and owner approval.
- **Technical:** clean audio, consistent loudness, seamless looping or suitable transitions, file size and browser-compatible format.
- **Accessibility/user control:** current mute/music controls must continue working.

## Outcomes and credits

**Proposed → Rights/technical reviewed → Creative review → Approved or declined → Separately implemented and tested.**

Approval of a proposal does not immediately add a track or promise a release. Accepted tracks receive agreed credit (artist name and track title, with applicable license/links) in documentation and in-game where implemented. Credits do not replace necessary copyright permissions.

A future curated playlist, switching logic, crossfades and track-selection UI are **not implemented**. Each requires separate scope approval, development, gameplay/audio testing and a controlled release. No existing music is replaced automatically.

The maintainer may decline any track for creative fit or rights reasons without implying that it lacks artistic merit.
